ACME Bank — Governance and People Report 2024

Board and Governance
The Board of Directors comprises 9 members, of whom 3 are women (33% female representation).
We recorded 2 customer privacy complaints substantiated during the year.

Workforce
New employee hires totalled 238 during 2024, with a voluntary turnover rate of 8%.
Anti-corruption training was completed by 100% of staff and board members.
