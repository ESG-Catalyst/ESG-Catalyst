ACME Bank — Environmental Data Sheet 2024

Greenhouse Gas Emissions
Our total Scope 1 direct GHG emissions were 1,250 tCO2e in 2024.
Scope 2 indirect emissions from purchased electricity were 3,400 tCO2e.
Combined Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions therefore totalled 4,650 tCO2e.

Energy and Water
Total energy consumption across all branches was 45,000 GJ.
Total water withdrawal was 12,000 cubic meters for the reporting year.
